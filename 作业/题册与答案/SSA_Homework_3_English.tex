\problem{3.14}{Required}{86}
Two zero-mean wide-sense stationary random processes $X(t)$ and $Y(t)$ have variances $\sigma_X^2=5$ and $\sigma_Y^2=10$. Can the following functions be their autocorrelation functions? Explain each answer. Here $u(\tau)$ denotes the unit step.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R_X(\tau)=5u(\tau)\exp(-3\tau)$.
\item $R_X(\tau)=5\sin(5\tau)$.
\item $R_Y(\tau)=9/(1+2\tau^2)$.
\item $R_Y(\tau)=-\cos(6\tau)\exp(-|\tau|)$.
\item $R_X(\tau)=5[\sin(3\tau)/(3\tau)]^2$.
\item $R_Y(\tau)=6+4\sin(10\tau)/(10\tau)$.
\item $R_X(\tau)=5\exp(-|\tau|)$.
\item $R_Y(\tau)=6+4\exp(-3\tau^2)$.
\end{enumerate}
Removable singularities are understood by their limits.
\answer

\problem{3.21}{Required}{86--87}
Which of the following functions can be power spectral densities of real random signals? Explain why or why not.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $[\sin\omega/\omega]^2$.
\item $\omega^2/(\omega^6+3\omega^2+3)$.
\item $\omega^2/(\omega^4-1)-\delta(\omega)$.
\item $\omega^4/(\jj\omega^6+\omega^2+1)$.
\item $|\omega|/(\omega^4+2\omega^2+1)$.
\item $\exp[-(\omega-1)^2]$.
\end{enumerate}
\answer
