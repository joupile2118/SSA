\problem{1.6}{Required}{34}
There are four batches of components. The first batch contains $2\,000$ components, of which $5\%$ are defective. The second batch contains $500$ components, of which $40\%$ are defective. The third and fourth batches each contain $1\,000$ components, with approximately $10\%$ defective. A batch is selected at random, and then a component is drawn at random from that batch. Find:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The probability that the selected component is defective.
\item The probability that the component came from the second batch, given that it is defective.
\end{enumerate}
\answer\newpage

$0$ & $0.07$ & $0.18$ & $0.15$ \\
$1$ & $0.08$ & $0.32$ & $0.20$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The joint distribution function and joint probability density function of $X$ and $Y$;
\item The marginal probability mass functions of $X$ and $Y$;

\end{enumerate}
